MultitrackStudio vs REAPER: price, licence, trial and platforms compared

Updated: 2026-11-07

MultitrackStudio vs REAPER is a common question for home recordists who want to pay once. Both are compact, low cost recorders from small vendors; they differ in how they price, how long you can try them and how far updates go.

Choose MultitrackStudio if you want a simpler recorder, lifetime free updates and a free Lite edition with no stated time limit; Standard costs €74 once. Choose REAPER if you want one fully featured edition, Linux support and a 60 day full trial; it costs $60 for personal use or small businesses and $225 commercially, with updates through version 8.99.

MultitrackStudio vs REAPER at a glance

In a MultitrackStudio vs REAPER comparison, the first difference is the pricing model. MultitrackStudio sells three levels of one program, Lite, Standard and Pro, so you pay for the features you need. REAPER sells one fully featured program at two prices, depending on who you are and how much you earn. Facts below come from each vendor's own pages, read on 7 November 2026.

Price and licence

MultitrackStudio Standard at €74 and Pro at €134 include VAT, and the buy page promises lifetime free updates. The licence key file works on all your computers. The detail of what each level adds is on our MultitrackStudio pricing page.

REAPER's discounted licence is $60 and applies to individuals using it for personal use, businesses with yearly gross revenue of $20,000 or less, and schools and non profits. Everyone else pays $225 for the commercial licence. Taxes are added, and a licence bought today gets free upgrades through version 8.99. Over a long ownership the open ended update promise from Bremmers Audio Design is the stronger guarantee, while REAPER gives all features for $60 to those who qualify.

Trying before you buy

The two trials work differently. MultitrackStudio Lite is free to try with no time limit stated by the vendor, but it caps you at three tracks and 30 minute tracks and leaves out several effects and export formats. REAPER's evaluation is fully functional for 60 days without registration.

If you only need to know whether your interface and plugins work, both trials answer that. If you want to finish a full project before paying, REAPER's model gives more room; if you record short demos with few tracks, Lite may carry you a long way at no cost.

Features and workflow

MultitrackStudio is built around a clear recorder workflow: a song folder, an Add Track menu, a mixer with effect returns and groups, and a Mix Down menu. It has its own instruments, Vocal Tuner pitch correction from Standard, touchscreen and pen support and MIDI 2.0. Plugin hosting covers CLAP, VST3, VST2 and AU with ARA 2.

REAPER is a broader production environment that runs on more platforms, including Linux. It suits users who want to customise nearly everything; MultitrackStudio suits users who want fewer choices between the microphone and the finished file. Our MultitrackStudio review goes through its features in detail.

Which one to pick

  • Pick MultitrackStudio Standard if you record one or two sources at a time, want pitch correction and MP3 export, and prefer lifetime updates.
  • Pick MultitrackStudio Pro if you need multichannel recording and full automation with lifetime updates for €134.
  • Pick REAPER if you need Linux, want every feature in one licence, or qualify for the $60 discounted licence and want a 60 day full trial.
  • Look further if neither fits; our MultitrackStudio alternatives page lists other recorders.

FAQ

Is MultitrackStudio cheaper than REAPER?

It depends on who you are. REAPER's discounted licence is $60 for personal users and small businesses, while MultitrackStudio Standard is €74 with VAT included. For commercial users REAPER is $225 against €134 for MultitrackStudio Pro.

Which has better updates, MultitrackStudio or REAPER?

MultitrackStudio promises lifetime free updates. A REAPER licence bought now covers free upgrades through version 8.99.

Does MultitrackStudio run on Linux like REAPER?

No. MultitrackStudio runs on Windows, Mac and iPad. REAPER offers Linux builds for Intel and ARM.

How long can I try each one?

MultitrackStudio Lite has no stated time limit but is capped at three tracks. REAPER offers a fully functional 60 day evaluation.

Do both support VST3 and CLAP plugins?

MultitrackStudio supports CLAP, VST3, VST2 and, on Mac, Audio Unit, with ARA 2. Check REAPER's own site for its current plugin support.
