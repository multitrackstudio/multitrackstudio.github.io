MultitrackStudio 11.4: what it is, what the three editions cost and where to start

Updated: 2026-11-07

MultitrackStudio is a multitrack audio and MIDI recording program for Windows and Mac made by Bremmers Audio Design. The free Lite edition records up to 3 tracks; Standard (€74) removes the track limit and Pro (€134) adds high sample rates, multichannel recording and full automation. Each licence is a one time purchase with lifetime free updates.

What MultitrackStudio does

MultitrackStudio turns a Windows PC or a Mac into a multitrack recorder. You can build a song one track at a time, playing along with what you already recorded, or record a whole band at once if your sound device has enough inputs. Audio and MIDI live side by side in the same song, so a vocal take, a guitar part and a MIDI piano can sit in one mixer.

The program is made by Bremmers Audio Design, a small Dutch developer that has sold it since 2001. The current release is 11.4.2, dated 20 October 2026 on the vendor's download page, where the free Lite installer is 16.8 MB for Windows and 18.8 MB for Mac.

The feature set covers the whole path from microphone to finished file: recording with punch in and loop takes, editing audio and MIDI (piano roll, score, drum and pattern editors), a mixer with unlimited effect return and group sections, built in effects and instruments, plugin hosting for VST, CLAP and Audio Unit, and a mixdown to a single audio file.

Three editions, one program

There is one installer and three levels of licence. Lite is the free level and its main limit is three tracks. Standard removes the track limit and the 30 minute track length cap and adds vocal pitch correction, the guitar amp, a master limiter and MP3, FLAC and M4A export. Pro adds what a project studio with a multichannel interface needs: up to 140 recording channels, 88.2 to 192 kHz recording, 24 bit and 32 bit float files, automation of every fader and knob, and external sidechaining.

Prices are €74 for Standard and €134 for Pro, VAT included, paid once. The full line between editions, the upgrade price and the refund terms are on the MultitrackStudio pricing page of this guide.

Who it suits

MultitrackStudio fits a home recordist who wants a complete recorder without a subscription, and who works on both Windows and Mac: the vendor says the licence key file works on all your computers and is not tied to one machine. It also suits people with a touchscreen or a Microsoft Surface, which have their own chapters in the manual, and MIDI users who want MIDI 2.0 support.

It is less of a fit if you need Linux, or if you already work in a large commercial DAW with a big ecosystem of tutorials. Our MultitrackStudio review weighs those limits, and the MultitrackStudio vs REAPER page compares it with the closest low cost rival.

Where to start

Start with Lite. It is the same program with a three track ceiling, enough to check that your sound device, microphone and MIDI keyboard work with it. Then follow the vendor's quick start, rewritten step by step on our how to record a song in MultitrackStudio page. If recording is silent or tracks drift out of sync, the MultitrackStudio troubleshooting page lists the documented fixes.

When you hit the track limit, buy Standard or Pro on the vendor's site. Payment runs through Paddle, and the vendor offers a 30 day money back guarantee.

  • Install Lite and set your sound device in Studio > Devices
  • Record a test song with a click track
  • Decide between Standard and Pro by the number of inputs you record at once
  • Buy once; updates are free for life

FAQ

Is MultitrackStudio free?

The Lite edition is free to download and try. Its main limit is three tracks, and it also caps track length at 30 minutes and leaves out pitch correction and MP3 export. Standard and Pro are paid once.

Who makes MultitrackStudio?

Bremmers Audio Design, a small developer whose site carries a 2001 to 2026 copyright. The program, the manual and the iPad version all come from that one vendor.

Does MultitrackStudio run on Mac?

Yes. The Mac version is a Universal Binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, and the vendor lists macOS 27 back to OS X 10.11. Plugins can be Audio Unit, CLAP, VST3 or VST2.

Is MultitrackStudio a subscription?

No. Standard costs €74 and Pro €134, once, VAT included. The buy page says every licence comes with lifetime free updates.

Can I use one licence on Windows and Mac?

The vendor says the licence key file works on all your computers and the program is not tied to one machine. The buy page lists Windows and Mac as covered platforms.

What is the latest version?

Version 11.4.2, dated 20 October 2026 on the vendor's download page. It added a 175% interface size, VST3 support for MIDI 2.0 per note controllers and better CPU efficiency on Windows on ARM.
