What the MultitrackStudio download includes

The MultitrackStudio download page offers MultitrackStudio Lite, the free level of the program, in two files: an .exe installer for Windows and a .zip for Mac. The same page has the PDF manual (1.9 MB) in English, German and Dutch, and a link to the free MultitrackStudio Remote app for iPad on the App Store.

Lite is the full program with limits. The vendor names three tracks as the main one; the buy page also shows that Lite caps tracks at 30 minutes and leaves out pitch correction, the guitar amp and MP3, FLAC and M4A export. Paying for Standard or Pro removes those limits, and the edition line is laid out on the MultitrackStudio pricing page.

Current version

The vendor lists version 11.4.2, dated 20 October 2026. Its release notes add a Send Report Now button in the Contact window, VST3 support for MIDI 2.0 per note controllers and the Orchestral Articulation profile, a 175% interface size and a choice of .mid or .midi2 in Export MIDI Tracks. They also mention better CPU efficiency on Windows on ARM and fixes for playback over Remote Desktop and some MIDI 2.0 USB devices on Mac.

Updates are free for life for paid licences, so the version you download today is the one buyers get as well.

Install and first run

  1. Download the right file: Click the download button and choose Windows (.exe) or Mac (.zip) on the vendor's page.
  2. Install: On Windows run the installer. On Mac unzip the file and move MultitrackStudio to the Applications folder.
  3. Set your devices: Open the Studio menu and choose Devices to select your sound device and MIDI devices. On Windows pick ASIO or the Windows driver type.
  4. Allow the microphone: On Windows 10 and 11, allow apps to access your microphone in Settings > Privacy, or recordings stay silent.
  5. Record a test song: Use Song > New, add a track from the Add Track menu, arm it with Rec and start the Transport.

The full procedure with click track and mixdown is on our how to record a song in MultitrackStudio page. Check first that your Windows or macOS version is on the MultitrackStudio system requirements list.

Trial and licence terms

Lite costs nothing and has no time limit stated on the vendor's pages; the limit is the feature set. When you buy Standard (€74) or Pro (€134), you receive a licence key file that the vendor says works on all your computers, plus download instructions by email. Updates are free for life and the buy page carries a 30 day money back guarantee.

Only download from multitrackstudio.com. Copies from other sites are not supported by the vendor, and the licence key is the only legitimate way to unlock the paid editions.

If the installer runs but recording does not work, see MultitrackStudio troubleshooting.

FAQ

Is the MultitrackStudio download free?

Yes. The download is MultitrackStudio Lite, which is free to try. Its main limit is three tracks; Standard and Pro are paid licences from the same site.

How big is the MultitrackStudio installer?

The Windows installer is 16.8 MB and the Mac zip is 18.8 MB, for version 11.4.2. The PDF manual is a separate 1.9 MB file.

Does Lite expire?

The vendor's pages do not mention a time limit for Lite. The restriction is on features: three tracks, 30 minute tracks and no MP3 export, among others.

How do I get Standard or Pro after buying?

The buy page says each licence comes with a licence key file, and paid users receive download instructions by email. The key works on all your computers.

Is there a MultitrackStudio app for iPad?

Yes. The download page links the free MultitrackStudio Remote app on the App Store, and the vendor also has a separate iPad version of MultitrackStudio whose songs can be opened in the desktop program.
