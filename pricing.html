MultitrackStudio price: Lite, Standard and Pro compared, with upgrade and refund terms

Updated: 2026-11-07

The MultitrackStudio price is €74 for Standard and €134 for Pro, paid once with VAT included, and Lite is free to try. Here is what each level unlocks, what the upgrade costs and what the vendor says about refunds.

MultitrackStudio Standard costs €74 and Pro costs €134, both one time payments with VAT included and lifetime free updates. Lite is free to try with a three track limit. Registered Standard users can move to Pro for €64, and the vendor offers a 30 day money back guarantee.

MultitrackStudio price at a glance

The MultitrackStudio price list on the vendor's buy page has two paid levels, both charged once. Standard is €74 and Pro is €134, and the vendor states that prices include VAT or sales tax. There is no subscription, no yearly renewal and no separate Mac price: the buy page lists Windows and Mac together.

The vendor publishes prices only in euro. Payment is handled by Paddle, which shows the amount at checkout; the buy page does not list dollar or pound prices, so this guide does not either.

What Standard adds over Lite

Lite is the free level, and the download page names its main limit: no more than three tracks. Standard lifts that and adds a long list of tools that a home recordist will notice quickly.

  • Unlimited tracks, and tracks longer than 30 minutes
  • Vocal pitch correction, audio transpose and the Transposer effect
  • Master Limiter, Guitar Amp and Tuner
  • Auto Wah, Convolutor, Dynamics, Exciter and Phaser effects
  • Time stretching and warping in the audio and MIDI editors
  • Matrix Sampler, punch in and sound on sound recording
  • Export to FLAC, M4A and MP3, plus sample rate conversion

What Pro adds over Standard

Pro is about bigger sessions and higher resolution. The tech specs limit lower editions to two simultaneous recording channels at 44.1 or 48 kHz into 16 bit files, although they can open higher quality files made elsewhere. Pro removes those limits.

  • Automation of all faders and rotary knobs
  • External sidechaining
  • Multichannel recording, up to 140 channels depending on the sound device
  • 24 bit, 32 bit and 32 bit float audio files
  • Recording at 88.2, 96, 176.4 and 192 kHz
  • Multitrack Editor and VariSpeed
  • Routing to sound device outputs and up to 4 MIDI in and out devices
  • Stem export, Multiband Compressor, Mid/Side Effect, Song Templates

A simple rule: count the inputs you record at the same moment. If it is one or two, Standard is enough. If it is more, or you want 96 kHz projects, buy Pro. Our MultitrackStudio review explains the trade off in more detail.

Licence terms

Each licence comes with a licence key file that, in the vendor's words, works for all your computers; the support page adds that the program is not tied to a computer. The buy page does not state a maximum number of machines, so none is given here. The licence is for the edition you buy, and every later version is a free update.

Upgrading from Standard to Pro costs €64 for registered users. The buy page says you order it by following the download instructions in the email you received with your licence. Mixed platforms are covered: the same purchase runs on Windows and Mac, and the MultitrackStudio system requirements page lists the versions supported.

Refunds, discounts and coupons

The buy page offers a 30 day money back guarantee. It does not describe the refund procedure, and the support page does not either; the vendor points to a contact form and a forum for questions, so use the contact page if you need to claim the guarantee.

The vendor publishes no educational discount, no coupon codes and no seasonal sale on its pages as of 7 November 2026. Codes offered on third party sites are not from the vendor; the only price you can rely on is the one shown at Paddle checkout. Lite is the free way to evaluate before paying.

Version and installer details are on the MultitrackStudio download page. If the price model does not suit you, the MultitrackStudio alternatives page lists other options.

FAQ

How much is MultitrackStudio?

Standard is €74 and Pro is €134, each paid once with VAT included. Lite is free to try with a three track limit. Prices were read on the vendor's buy page on 7 November 2026.

Is MultitrackStudio a one time purchase?

Yes. Both paid editions are one time payments, and the vendor says every licence includes lifetime free updates. There is no subscription.

How much does the upgrade from Standard to Pro cost?

€64 for registered Standard users. The buy page says you order it through the download instructions sent by email with your licence.

Does MultitrackStudio have a refund policy?

The vendor offers a 30 day money back guarantee on its buy page. The steps for claiming it are not published, so contact the vendor through the contact page on multitrackstudio.com.

Is there a MultitrackStudio coupon or student discount?

The vendor's pages list no coupon codes and no educational discount as of 7 November 2026. The free Lite edition is the vendor's way to try before buying.

Can I pay in dollars?

The vendor lists prices in euro only. Payment goes through Paddle, which handles the payment options at checkout.
