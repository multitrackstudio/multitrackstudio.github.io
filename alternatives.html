MultitrackStudio alternatives: other multitrack recorders for Windows, Mac and Linux

Updated: 2026-11-07

MultitrackStudio alternatives worth a look if you need Linux, a free program with no track limit, or a larger production DAW. Each entry links to its own vendor; check prices there, since this page only reports what MultitrackStudio's vendor publishes.

The closest MultitrackStudio alternative is REAPER, a low cost DAW that also runs on Linux. For free recording, Audacity covers audio editing on every desktop system and GarageBand covers Mac users. Ardour suits Linux users who want an open source DAW, and Cakewalk, Mixcraft and Waveform suit Windows users who want a larger program.

Why look for MultitrackStudio alternatives

People search for MultitrackStudio alternatives for a few clear reasons. Lite stops at three tracks. Standard records only two inputs at once at 44.1 or 48 kHz. There is no Linux version. And some users want a bigger program with more tutorials and third party content.

If none of those apply to you, MultitrackStudio itself may be the answer: Standard is €74 once with lifetime updates, as the MultitrackStudio pricing page shows. If one does, the list below sorts the options by what they solve.

The alternatives

  • REAPER: The closest match on price and size. One fully featured edition, a 60 day full trial and builds for Windows, Mac and Linux.
  • Audacity: Free, open source audio recorder and editor for Windows, Mac and Linux. Good for recording and editing audio, with no MIDI sequencing to speak of.
  • Ardour: Open source DAW for Linux, Mac and Windows, aimed at multichannel recording and mixing.
  • GarageBand: Apple's free recorder for Mac, with instruments and loops. Mac only.
  • Tracktion Waveform Free: A free edition of Tracktion's DAW for Windows, Mac and Linux, with plugin hosting.
  • Cakewalk by BandLab: A long running Windows DAW now developed under BandLab; check its site for current editions and terms.
  • Mixcraft: A Windows recorder from Acoustica aimed at home users, sold in several editions.

When MultitrackStudio is still the better pick

MultitrackStudio holds up well against these options in a few areas. The price is paid once and the vendor promises lifetime free updates. The licence key works on all your computers, Windows and Mac. It hosts CLAP, VST3, VST2 and AU plugins with ARA 2, supports MIDI 2.0, and runs natively on Apple Silicon and Windows on ARM. Touchscreen and pen input have their own chapters in the manual.

The MultitrackStudio review covers its strengths and limits in one place.

  • Same licence on Windows and Mac
  • Native ARM builds on both platforms
  • Free Lite edition to test your hardware

FAQ

What is the best free alternative to MultitrackStudio?

It depends on your system. Audacity is free on Windows, Mac and Linux for audio recording and editing; GarageBand is free on Mac with instruments; Waveform Free is a free DAW on all three systems.

Is there a MultitrackStudio alternative for Linux?

Yes. REAPER, Ardour and Audacity all run on Linux. MultitrackStudio itself is Windows, Mac and iPad only.

Is REAPER better than MultitrackStudio?

Neither is better for everyone. REAPER has one fully featured edition, Linux support and a 60 day trial; MultitrackStudio has a simpler workflow, lifetime updates and a free Lite edition.

Why choose MultitrackStudio over a free program?

The paid editions add unlimited tracks, pitch correction, MP3 export and, in Pro, multichannel recording and automation, for a one time price with lifetime updates. Lite lets you test it first at no cost.
