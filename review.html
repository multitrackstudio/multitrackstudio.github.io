MultitrackStudio scores 7.6 out of 10 in this review. It covers recording, MIDI, editing, mixing and mixdown for a one time €74 (Standard) or €134 (Pro) with lifetime free updates and one key for all your computers. The limits are clear: Standard records only two channels at 44.1 or 48 kHz, and there is no Linux version.

Verdict (7.6/10)

For a songwriter recording one or two sources at a time, Standard at €74 is a complete recorder with pitch correction, MP3 export and free updates for life. Buy Pro only if your interface records more than two inputs at once, if you want 96 kHz or 24 bit files, or if you need full automation.

MultitrackStudio review in short

In this MultitrackStudio review the score rests on what the vendor documents, not on lab tests. The program records audio and MIDI, edits both, mixes with built in effects and plugins, and renders a final file. It runs on Windows 11 to 7 (64 bit, 32 bit and native Windows on ARM) and on macOS as a Universal Binary for Apple Silicon and Intel.

Two things stand out. First, the price model: Standard is €74 and Pro €134, paid once, and the buy page promises lifetime free updates. Second, the licence key file is not tied to a computer, so a laptop and a desktop, or a PC and a Mac, run on the same purchase. The trade off is a strict line between editions, which the MultitrackStudio pricing page lays out row by row.

Features

  • Recording: Multiple, alternate and partial takes, punch in and out, loop and sound on sound recording, click track and a practice mode.
  • MIDI: Piano roll, score, drum and pattern editors, built in General MIDI instruments, sampler, SoundFont player, MIDI 2.0 and MIDI-CI.
  • Effects: Compressor, EQ, reverb, echo, deesser, Vocal Tuner and more, plus VST, CLAP and AU plugins with ARA 2 and delay compensation.
  • Mixing: Unlimited effect return and group sections, mixer section templates and a master section with dither and noise shaping.
  • Touch and remote: Touchscreen and pen support, control surfaces and a free iPad remote app.

Standard or Pro

Standard is where most home users should land. Compared with Lite it removes the three track ceiling and the 30 minute track length cap, and adds vocal pitch correction, the guitar amp and tuner, a master limiter, time stretching in the editors and export to MP3, FLAC and M4A.

Pro is for a different setup. The tech specs give lower editions two simultaneous recording channels at 44.1 or 48 kHz into 16 bit files. Pro raises that to up to 140 channels, depending on the interface, sample rates up to 192 kHz and 24 bit, 32 bit and 32 bit float files. It also adds automation of every fader and rotary knob, external sidechaining, the Multitrack Editor, VariSpeed, stem export and the multiband compressor. If you record a drum kit with eight microphones, you need Pro; a singer with a guitar does not.

Platform support and plugins

Plugin support is broad for a program this size: CLAP, VST3 and VST2 on Windows, plus Audio Unit v2 and v3 on Mac, with ARA 2 for CLAP, VST3 and AU. On Windows on ARM the program is built as ARM64EC, so existing x64 plugins still load. The audio engine uses up to eight CPU cores and processes in 32 bit.

Driver support on Windows covers ASIO, WASAPI, MME and DirectSound; on Mac it is Core Audio. The full list, including the macOS version range, is on the MultitrackStudio system requirements page.

FAQ

Is MultitrackStudio good for beginners?

The vendor's quick start takes you from an empty song to a mixed file in about ten steps, and the manual is available online and as a PDF in English, German and Dutch. The interface is one window with a mixer and tracks, so there is less to learn than in larger DAWs.

Is MultitrackStudio Pro worth it over Standard?

Only if you need what Pro adds: more than two inputs at once, sample rates above 48 kHz, 24 bit or 32 bit float files, full automation or external sidechaining. Otherwise Standard at €74 covers a typical home recording setup.

Does MultitrackStudio support VST3 and CLAP?

Yes. Windows versions host CLAP, VST3 and VST2 plugins, and the Mac version adds Audio Unit v2 and v3. ARA 2 is supported for CLAP, VST3 and AU.

Does MultitrackStudio have pitch correction?

Yes, from Standard up. Vocal pitch correction and the Vocal Tuner effect are listed among the features Standard adds over Lite.

Are there independent tests in this review?

No. This review is based on the vendor's own feature list, tech specs and prices, read on 7 November 2026. Try Lite on your own hardware before buying.
